A Small Business With a Real Data Problem

Our client operates a small trucking business with two drivers. Delivery assignments are received through a dispatcher. The business transports products to delivery locations while tracking mileage, fuel, time, expenses, and revenue.

Current reality

Initial understanding
  • Two-person trucking operation
  • Delivery work received through a dispatcher
  • Mileage and deliveries recorded manually
  • Fuel and business receipts kept separately
  • Logbooks used throughout the workday
  • Profitability is difficult to evaluate quickly
  1. Dispatch order Assignment arrives from the dispatcher
  2. Delivery Miles, fuel, and time build up on the road
  3. Paper records Notebooks, receipts, and logbooks in separate places
  4. Financial reporting Records are pieced together, often at tax time

The Trigger Tax season.

Scattered or incomplete records can create additional costs during tax preparation. The client also needs a clearer way to understand expenses, revenue, mileage, and the profitability of individual deliveries.

Our design question

How might we help a small trucking owner-operator capture operational and financial information accurately without making the daily workflow more complicated?

  1. Manual entry
  2. Scattered records
  3. Missing information
  4. Reporting difficulty
  5. Additional cost

What We Will and Won't Build

Project goal

Our project will help VMR Trucking track daily load deliveries and store shipment information as it happens, so everything needed for taxes is ready at the end of the year. We'll know we succeeded when bookkeeping becomes faster and more accurate, and the solution should also work for other small trucking businesses that still keep their books on paper.

  • Mirrors the two books

    Digital versions of the logbook and the accounts book, so the move from paper to digital feels familiar, simple, and quick.

  • Multiple languages

    We plan to offer the tool in different languages so more owner-operators can use it comfortably.

  • Beyond one client

    Built for anyone who does their bookkeeping on paper or without electronic storage.

    Assumption Many small trucking businesses work under dispatchers and brokers across the state.

Out of scope

If a request lands here, the answer is no for this project.

  • Dispatch integration. Orders from the dispatcher are not connected to the tool.
  • Tax calculation. We organize the records needed at tax time; we don't calculate the business's taxes.
  • Delivery planning and delays. No predicted delivery plans, and no tracking of pickups missed because of delays. Tentative

The People Around the Load

Three people whose workday shapes how a delivery gets done. Select a persona to meet them.

Drafted September 29, 2026

Dispatcher / broker

Joseph

A focused manager whose daily goal is making sure every load is assigned and every delivery is completed.

Goals

  • Assign all of the day’s load pickups.
  • Distribute shipments fairly, so every carrier gets at least one high-demand assignment.
  • Give each load to a dependable, experienced carrier.

Frustrations

  • Low-paying load assignments.
  • Concerns about favoritism.
  • Complaints from operators who feel they aren’t getting the best shipments.

Success looks like

Operators are content, and complaints go down.

Truck driver

Helga

Delivers shipments to and from warehouses across California.

Goals

  • Deliver every shipment on time.
  • Complete at least two deliveries a day.

Frustrations

  • Loading and unloading delays at warehouses.
  • Truck maintenance and repairs.
  • High diesel costs.

Success looks like

Making three deliveries and getting home before 1:00 PM. That usually happens when warehouse delays are minimal, or the shipment is already loaded for a quick swap.

Warehouse worker

Arnulfo

Loads and unloads trucks as trailers back up to the dock, then moves the goods inside the warehouse.

Goals

  • Unload trucks quickly.

Frustrations

  • Forklift breakdowns in the middle of loading or unloading.

Success looks like

Loading and unloading go smoothly, with no problems.
