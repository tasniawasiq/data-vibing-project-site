Communication & Rules

Protocols for Team Alignment

Platform & Response

Platform: Group Text / iMessage

Response Time: Before end of day (10:00 PM) unless deadline is tight; response within a couple of hours during rush periods.

Meetings & Decisions

Meetings: Friday @ 5:30 / 6:00 PM (Zoom). Scheduling conflicts managed via group text.

Decision-Making: Majority vote wins. Disagreements resolved through constructive debate or re-voting.

Participation & Deadlines: Attend all Zoom meetings, respond promptly, and complete assigned tasks on schedule. Ensure the team meets at least once before any class or internal deliverable deadline.
🤝

Having Our Backs

Accountability & Support

We maintain mutual accountability by providing reasonable advance notice through our group message when personal scheduling conflicts arise.

Unresponsive Teammate Protocol

Send a direct message through group chat, followed by direct follow-up messages from other team members. Most concerns are handled internally via phone conversation or group text.

Instructor Escalation

If a teammate is non-responsive for more than 1 week (e.g., missing class one week and failing to respond into the new week), we will loop in the instructor.
